**# AI-Assisted Copywriting Sample

## Project Overview

This portfolio sample demonstrates how I use generative AI as a drafting tool while applying human judgment to improve brand voice, cultural relevance, accuracy, readability, and audience engagement.

The sample was created for an AI-powered matchmaking yacht event targeting college students and young adults in Los Angeles.

> **Portfolio Disclosure:** This is an independently created portfolio sample. It was not published as an official post for any company.

## Project Details

- **Platform:** Instagram
- **Target Audience:** College students and young adults in Los Angeles
- **Objective:** Encourage qualified users to apply for an AI-powered yacht matchmaking event
- **AI Tool:** ChatGPT
- **My Role:** Prompt development, copy editing, brand-voice adaptation, cultural review, and final approval

## 1. Original Prompt

```text
Write an Instagram caption promoting an upcoming AI-powered yacht matchmaking event in Los Angeles.

The service uses AI to match college students and helps them meet offline instead of endlessly swiping on dating apps. The target audience is Gen Z college students in Los Angeles.

The caption should:
- Have a fun, youthful, and energetic tone;
- Start with a strong opening hook;
- Explain that the service handles the matching;
- Emphasize real-life connections and no swiping;
- Include a call to action encouraging users to apply through the link in bio;
- Be fewer than 120 words.
```

## 4. Editing Rationale

### Stronger Opening Hook

The AI-generated opening, “Ready to meet your perfect match?”, sounded generic and similar to traditional dating-app advertising. I replaced it with “your dating apps had their chance” to establish a clearer anti-swiping position and capture attention immediately.

### More Natural Brand Voice

I replaced long promotional sentences with shorter lines, conversational language, and lowercase styling. This makes the caption feel more natural, confident, and appropriate for a Gen Z audience.

### Removal of Unsupported Promises

The original draft used phrases such as “perfect match” and “looking for love,” which could overpromise the outcome of AI matchmaking. The revised version focuses on the event experience and the opportunity to meet someone offline.

### Clearer Product Value

The line “tell us your type. we’ll handle the match” explains the role of the AI matchmaking service without adding unnecessary technical details.

### Improved Mobile Readability

The final caption uses short paragraphs and intentional line breaks, making it easier to read on Instagram and other mobile-first platforms.

### Stronger Engagement Strategy

In addition to the application CTA, I added a tagging prompt to encourage comments, shares, and organic engagement without making the caption feel overly promotional.

## 5. Human Review Checklist

Before publication, I would verify:

- Event date, location, eligibility, and application deadline;
- Accuracy of all event benefits and participation requirements;
- Brand voice and platform-specific formatting;
- Image, music, and content usage rights;
- Privacy requirements for attendee information;
- Applicable advertising and AI-content disclosure requirements.**
