# Tencent Ads Campaign Optimization

## Project Overview

This team project developed and executed a full-funnel digital advertising campaign for the Communication University of China Media Museum.

The campaign combined audience research, competitor analysis, creative testing, landing-page optimization, audience targeting, and performance analysis. Our team used campaign data to identify weaknesses in the initial creatives and improve the final advertising strategy.

> **Project Type:** Team Project  
> **Platform:** Tencent Ads  
> **Campaign Objective:** Increase awareness of the museum and generate new followers for its WeChat Official Account

## Campaign Background

The Communication University of China Media Museum presents the history and development of broadcasting, television, film, and media technology.

Our preliminary research identified several marketing challenges:

- Limited awareness among potential visitors;
- Lack of a mature promotional strategy;
- Broad audience groups without sufficiently targeted messaging;
- Weak interaction between users and existing promotional content;
- A long conversion path between the advertisement and the follow action.

## Target Audiences

The campaign focused on:

- Students and faculty at the Communication University of China;
- College students interested in museums, exhibitions, and social-media-friendly destinations;
- Beijing residents looking for weekend cultural activities;
- Students interested in media, communication, film, and broadcasting;
- People interested in visiting museums online through virtual exhibitions.

## Research and Strategy

Before launching the campaign, our team conducted:

- Audience and user-needs analysis;
- Social media and review analysis;
- Competitor research;
- PEST analysis of the museum industry;
- Campaign objective development;
- Audience-targeting and bidding planning;
- Creative concept and landing-page development.

The campaign promoted both the museum’s physical exhibitions and its online virtual-reality exhibition experience.

## Creative Testing Process

### Version 1: Purple Creative

The first version used a colorful visual style but did not communicate the museum theme clearly enough.

The second version used a retro collage style that better matched the museum’s historical identity.

## Key Findings

The testing process identified four main problems:

1. The museum name and central campaign message were not prominent enough in the external ad creative.
2. The visual style of the advertisement did not fully match the landing page.
3. The landing page contained too much text and required users to scroll too far before seeing the conversion button.
4. The original audience targeting and bidding strategy limited campaign reach.

## Campaign Optimization

Based on the test results, the final campaign made the following changes:

- Increased the visual prominence of the museum name;
- Used a retro red-brown design that better reflected the museum’s brand identity;
- Aligned the visual style of the external advertisement and internal landing page;
- Shortened the landing page and simplified its messaging;
- Added a conversion button near the beginning of the landing page;
- Added additional CTA opportunities throughout the page;
- Refined audience segments and bidding decisions based on early performance;
- Expanded geographic targeting to selected major cities.

## Final Campaign Results

| Metric | Final Result |
|---|---:|
| Ad Spend | RMB 2,836.76 |
| Impressions | 143,434 |
| Average CTR | 1.69% |
| Peak CTR | 2.69% |
| Average CPC | RMB 1.49 |
| New WeChat Followers | 254 |
| Cost per Follower | RMB 12.12 |
| Follow Conversion Rate | 12.32% |

The optimized campaign increased average CTR from **0.79% to 1.69%**, while the peak CTR reached **2.69%**.

It also reduced the follower acquisition cost compared with the initial creative and generated **254 new WeChat Official Account followers**.

## Key Takeaways

### Creative Consistency Matters

Using a consistent visual identity across the external advertisement and landing page created a smoother user experience and strengthened the campaign message.

### Clear Message Hierarchy Improves Performance

Making the museum name more prominent helped users understand the purpose of the advertisement immediately.

### Shorter Conversion Paths Increase Action

Moving the follow button closer to the beginning of the landing page reduced the effort required to complete the desired action.

### Testing Should Inform Audience Strategy

Early performance data helped the team refine audience targeting, geographic reach, and bidding decisions before the formal campaign launch.
